About

Engineering leadership, end to end

I've spent 25+ years in engineering, the last decade of it leading organizations rather than codebases. My strengths are engineering org design, scaling and cross-region team leadership, hiring and bar-raising, M&A technical integration, and platform strategy, with a consistent record of strong team health and above-average eNPS.

That track record spans finance, e-commerce, food delivery, social media, advertising, and blockchain: institutional digital asset custody at Deutsche Bank, platform leadership at Delivery Hero and HelloFresh. I go deep in regulated fintech: blockchain and tokenization, MiCA/BaFin/AML compliance, and company-wide GraphQL API standards, on a hands-on background across Java, Go, Python, Ruby, Node.js and PHP, on AWS, GCP and on-prem.

Executive engineering roles across six companies, 200+ interviews conducted, 80+ engineers hired, and 10+ former direct reports since promoted into Director, Head-of and VP roles.
